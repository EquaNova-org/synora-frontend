Synora Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Synora ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website, application, and related services (the "Service").

Please read this carefully. Synora is not a licensed healthcare provider, and using the Service does not create a doctor-patient relationship. The Service is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.

Synora may in the future offer additional products or device integrations that are subject to separate regulatory approval as medical devices; any such product will have its own applicable privacy terms, provided at the time it becomes available. This Policy describes the Service as it exists today.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Questions and messages you type into the chat interface.
  • Documents you upload (e.g., PDF files) and their extracted content.
  • Your selected role/audience (individual, employee, or doctor/nurse) when using the Service.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Basic technical logs (timestamps, request outcomes, response times) used to operate and debug the Service.
  • We do not currently use tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics trackers.

1.3 What We Do Not Collect (Today)

As of this version of the Service, we do not require account registration, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or payment information. If this changes (for example, when paid tiers launch), this Policy will be updated before that data collection begins, and your consent will be requested where required.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to: - Generate a response to your question using our AI pipeline (retrieval, analysis, and recommendation). - Improve the reliability and safety of the Service (e.g., reviewing logs to fix errors). - Operate essential safety features, such as detecting language associated with medical emergencies or crisis situations, in order to direct you to appropriate resources.

We do not use your health-related questions or uploaded documents to sell products, serve advertising, or build advertising profiles.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (for users in the EU/UK/EEA)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") or UK GDPR applies, our legal basis for processing your information is:

  • Explicit consent — because health-related information you may share (such as symptoms) is treated as "special category data" under Article 9 of the GDPR, we rely on your explicit, informed consent to process it. By typing a health-related question into the Service or uploading a document, you consent to that information being processed as described in this Policy.
  • Legitimate interest — for basic technical logs needed to operate, secure, and debug the Service.

You may withdraw consent at any time by discontinuing use of the Service and requesting deletion of your data (see Section 7).

4. How Your Information Is Processed — Third Parties

To provide the Service, your information is processed by the following categories of third-party infrastructure providers, strictly as needed to operate the Service:

Provider Purpose Data Involved
Groq, Inc. Runs the AI language models that generate responses Your questions and retrieved document context are sent to Groq's API for processing

We do not sell your information to these or any other third parties. These providers process data solely to help us operate the Service and are bound by their own privacy and security obligations.

5. Data Retention

  • Uploaded documents: stored in a session-specific, isolated database collection tied to a temporary session identifier. Uploaded documents are not linked to your identity and are retained only as long as needed to support your session, after which they may be periodically deleted.
  • Chat questions and responses: not currently stored in a persistent, per-user history. Basic operational logs (which may include the text of your question, for debugging purposes) are retained for a limited period for reliability and safety monitoring, then deleted or anonymized.
  • If you would like your uploaded documents or session data deleted sooner, contact us using the details in Section 10.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including: - API access controls to prevent unauthorized use of the Service. - Rate limiting to reduce abuse. - Restricting data processing to reputable infrastructure providers (Section 4).

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to: - Access the information we hold about you. - Request deletion of your information. - Object to or restrict certain processing. - Withdraw consent at any time (see Section 3). - Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (for EU/UK/EEA users).

We aim to honor these rights for all users worldwide, regardless of location, as a matter of good practice, even where not strictly legally required.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at, and should not be used by, individuals under the age of 16 (or the minimum age required by your local law). We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the Service, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. International Data Transfers

Because our infrastructure providers (Section 4) may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, your information may be transferred internationally. Where required by law, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for such transfers.
